# Detailed estimate input

Detailed estimates are deterministic, internal producer inputs. QwenWork scopes work; the engine does arithmetic and availability checks. A work line represents a cost-bearing task exactly once. The legacy package estimate is a separate path and must not be combined with these lines. Amounts ending in `_cents` are integer Singapore cents; dates and times are studio-local ISO dates/times. Record assumptions and cite a dated producer or catalog basis for estimates.

The shared application evaluator now defaults to detailed estimating when `detailed_spec` is omitted. It expands a founder-registered template or returns a specific `clarifications` entry with `resolver` and `field`; it never silently uses package pricing. Register an immutable template through the owner-only `/api/estimating/promo-template` route with `version`, `basis_date`, `note`, `supported` scope values, `rate` (cost/sell cents), `buffer_pct`, and positive `hours` for `prep`, `shoot`, `ingest`, `review_selected`, `review_per_footage_minute`, `original_edit`, `cutdown`, `captions`, `review_round`, and `delivery`. No production effort or hourly rates are shipped as purported Alex approval. Synthetic acceptance tests register their own labeled approval. Populate actual resource availability for `promo_producer`; absence is a schedule shortfall, not an invented calendar.

Buyer fact names shared with conversation are `production.original_edits`, `production.cutdowns`, `production.clips_preselected`, `production.footage_minutes`, `production.deadline_kind`, `production.first_cut_date`, and `production.final_delivery_date`. Supply the same names under `production_facts` for structured/MCP intake. Inquiry pricing overlays current confirmed facts and rejects conflicts. A first-cut deadline requires an explicit final date matching the offer deadline. Original edits and derivatives have distinct lines; supplied footage removes the shoot but keeps ingest and review. Raw footage quantity changes review effort only according to the registered template.

Use `pricing_mode: legacy_replay` (or the existing explicit `historical_pricing: false`) for pinned fixture replay; `pricing_mode: historical_replay` explicitly enables the old historical benchmark. Stored evaluations and published/confirmed terms are not rewritten. Clarified structured intake retains the same deal and creates a fresh offer/evaluation; inquiry corrections use the existing revision protocol. Detailed bookings require incremental detailed amendments and resource schedules, never legacy add-on arithmetic.

Add `detailed_spec` to an otherwise complete existing `offer_request` to bypass template expansion with explicit founder-supplied lines. The offer's `direct_costs_sgd` must equal the sum of expense `cost_cents` divided by 100. Schedule tasks must cover buffered planning hours and finish by the offer deadline. Populate the resource calendar first via the founder `/estimating` page. The same immutable scope and task inputs are saved in the evaluation and rechecked when confirming.

## Production scoping controls

Optional `setups` group shared work: `{"id", "kind": "shoot"|"post", "location"?, "date"?, "min_call_hours"?}`. A work item references a setup with `setup_id`. A shoot setup must map to exactly one contiguous shoot task (`kind: "shoot"` or `contiguous: true`) meeting its minimum call period — three reels sharing one shoot therefore carry one cost-bearing shoot task. Items marked `supplied: true` represent client-supplied footage; they may carry ingest/edit effort but can never join a shoot setup. Items may list the `deliverables` they serve and `evidence` links (`{"kind": "observation"|"fact", "id"}`); when pricing through `price_inquiry_scope`, every cited observation or fact must belong to that inquiry. Items may declare `depends_on` other item IDs (for example a cutdown depending on its master); unknown or cyclic dependencies are rejected, including inside alternatives.

Schedule tasks may declare `item_ids`. When any task does, every work line must be covered by tasks whose minutes sum to at least that line's buffered hours — the estimator rejects underallocated individual lines (`task_underallocated:<id>`), not only insufficient totals. A multi-line task apportions its minutes by line effort rather than crediting the full block to each line. Tasks without `item_ids` keep the aggregate coverage rule for existing unphased explicit specs. Finished phased promises require deliverable-linked lines and first-phase coverage of all applicable work for the specific `deliverable_ids` (default `video-1`, etc.), including edits, captions, review and delivery; prep/filming alone is rejected. A task's `setup_id` must reference a declared setup.

Producer controls: `save_producer_override` records a dated replacement for one line's `quantity` or `unit_hours` (idempotent per `request_id`, optional `supersedes`); `estimate_scope` with `apply_overrides: true` applies the latest override per line/field and rewrites the line basis to `producer override <date>`. `merge_scope_setups` combines compatible setups (shoot setups only when location and date agree) and `split_scope_setup` partitions a setup's items into groups; tagged tasks must declare `item_ids` that fit wholly inside one group. Both return a transformed spec to preview and save — they never invent effort. The founder `/estimating` page exposes the same operations at `/api/estimating/merge-setups`, `/api/estimating/split-setup`, `/api/estimating/overrides` and `/api/estimating/apply-overrides`.

Optional `low_unit_hours`, `high_unit_hours` and `assumption` on a line express scenarios, not probabilities. `propose_scope_alternatives(spec, offer, as_of, transformations)` (MCP and founder `/api/estimating/alternatives`) detects structural opportunities and builds consistent variants from producer-supplied transformations: `master_to_derivative` (quantity or distinct-item mode), `combine_shoots` (same location and date, producer-supplied combined call minutes), `phase_delivery` (dependency-closed first phase) and `use_supplied_footage` (producer-supplied ingest effort, optional expense removal). Effort values and dated bases are required inputs; schedule coverage is rebalanced mechanically and every variant is validated before return. Each accepted alternative carries a ready-to-price `variant`; rejected candidates carry reasons. For alternatives, add `detailed_alternatives` to the request with up to four complete `{ "offer": { ...overrides... }, "spec": { ...complete task set... } }` objects. Allowed overrides are `deliverables`, `caption_versions`, `deadline`, `budget_sgd`, and `direct_costs_sgd`. Each alternative is independently evaluated; an invalid alternate spec returns `clarify` naming the variant index, while commercially weaker variants are skipped. Item-level `depends_on` is enforced inside every variant, so a derivative whose master was removed cannot be priced. To phase, add two `phases` with counts and deadlines, and `task_ids` on the first phase; its dependencies must also be in that phase. The founder supplies work and dates; the software does not invent them.

Historical pricing is available only in explicit `pricing_mode: historical_replay`; set `historical_pricing: false` or `pricing_mode: legacy_replay` to replay the pinned demo calculation. It requires two exact scope-family matches completed before the quote and records their IDs and fees. Historical fees are revenue evidence, not labour cost or true margin. Eligible completed actuals also contribute to the engine effort comparables. Captioned projects can inform fees but are excluded from baseline effort comparisons. Each fee benchmark records leave-one-out prices. For holdout scoring of supplied quote-time predictions, run `python3 scripts/backtest_estimates.py INPUT.json --train-through YYYY-MM-DD`; its input contract is in the script. It does not train a model or reconstruct unrecorded costs.

For the rate-card, history and detailed comparison, provide `predictions` with `ratecard`, `history` and `detailed` hour estimates for each held-out project. Include `campaign_id` when projects share a campaign; a campaign spanning the cutoff is rejected. Cost errors are reported only for cases with both actual and predicted costs. Predictions must be generated using only information available at quote time and history completed by then; the scorer cannot verify their provenance. The alternative `brief`, `detailed_brief`, `detailed_reference` keys remain supported for testing the value of reference evidence. These are synthetic or supplied results, not evidence of accuracy until completed jobs have been measured.

The founder booking preview layers the candidate's proposed resource blocks over the current calendar and checks each other published option independently. It reports weekly and resource conflicts without saving reservations; confirmation repeats the check in the booking transaction. A conflict reflects the scheduler's deterministic proposed blocks, not proof that no other arrangement exists.

Calculated additional-work orders freeze their rate inputs at creation and apply to the buyer-selected booking. Detailed amendments use a fresh catalog-validated incremental estimate, founder-supplied description and dated resource tasks. Approval binds the exact terms; confirmation rechecks and reserves the resources. The legacy add-on calculator refuses detailed bookings. Reliable closeouts include confirmed, calculable additions in their scope and fee; manually described changes are excluded from historical benchmarks.
